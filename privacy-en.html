MuscleIQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains how the MuscleIQ app for Android handles your information.

In short

  • Everything you enter stays on your phone.
  • No account, no server, no internet connection.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • We collect nothing and share nothing.

What the app stores

The app saves only what you enter or record in it:

  • Profile: age (saved as your birth year), sex, height, weight, body-fat percentage (optional), goal, experience level, training days, the equipment you have, your daily activity outside training, and any injuries you choose (joints or muscle groups).
  • Training: your program, workout sessions, sets (weight, reps, reps in reserve, set type), notes you type, and personal records.
  • Body and food: your weigh-ins with their dates, and your food log (foods, amounts, meals, and their calories and macronutrients).
  • Settings: language, theme and similar preferences.

Most fields are optional. You decide what to enter.

Where your data is kept

All of it is kept in a local database on your device. There is no account and no server, and the app does not connect to the internet: the published app does not even ask for network access.

Your data is also excluded from Android’s automatic cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer, so it is not copied anywhere unless you do it yourself.

What we collect or share: nothing

We, the developer, do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data. The app has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting, and no third-party software that collects data.

Google Play, where you get the app, handles its own data under Google’s privacy policy. Google may give developers anonymous, aggregated statistics, such as the number of installs and how stable the app is.

Backups you create

Export your log (Profile › Your data) creates a file (JSON) with all your data from the app. You choose where to save it, for example your phone’s storage or a cloud drive you use.

  • The file is not encrypted. Anyone who has it can read it, so keep it somewhere safe.
  • If you save it to a cloud service, that service’s own terms apply.
  • Import a backup replaces the data on this device with the contents of the file.

The app never sends this file anywhere by itself.

Deleting your data

Your data exists only on your device and in any backup files you made, so you are in control of it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your phone’s settings, permanently erases everything. Backup files you saved have to be deleted separately.

We cannot recover deleted data, and there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Health disclaimer

MuscleIQ gives general fitness and nutrition information, such as training volume and calorie and macronutrient estimates. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Talk to a doctor or another qualified professional before you start a new training or diet plan, especially if you have a health condition or an injury, or during pregnancy. Stop exercising if you feel pain, dizziness or chest discomfort.

Children

MuscleIQ is meant for adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and its calorie estimates are available only for ages 18 to 75. Because the app collects no data, we do not collect information from anyone, child or adult.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the developer contact details on MuscleIQ’s page on Google Play.
